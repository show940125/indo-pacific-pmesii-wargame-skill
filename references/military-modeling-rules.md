# Military Modeling Rules

V4 represents military capability as model-level inventory bands plus strategic interaction classes.

Rules:

- Use quantity bands, not exact tactical availability.
- Keep platform model, family, domain, role, readiness band, source id, and confidence.
- Model interactions such as air defense vs aircraft, submarine vs surface fleet, anti-ship missile vs naval task force, UAV saturation vs air defense, and cyber vs C4ISR.
- Gemini actors may propose actions only when the concrete actor has a matching capability or the controller records a violation.
- Codex explains military effects as PMESII deltas and strategic pressure, not deterministic combat outcomes.
